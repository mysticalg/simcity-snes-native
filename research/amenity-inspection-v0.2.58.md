# v0.2.58 — selected amenity benefits and maximum land value

Scoped player requests: selecting a park/plaza should highlight helped zones and quantify land/commercial growth contributions; existing expensive parks should be able to max nearby land value without re-plopping.

The previous combined amenity cap of +85 held districts below maximum even with many expensive amenities. The land field now permits a combined +250 while final land value remains capped at250. Original distance falloff, reach, operation, upkeep and global commerce cap (+720 local score) remain. The cap is a Turbo balance change, not a ROM assertion. Existing plots are discovered from loaded map state; no replacement is needed. Parks now recognise both road and rail access, avoiding inactive rail-only districts. The map legend incorrectly described green as low land value; it now correctly says green high / red low and shows the hovered 0–250 value.

Select/inspect a park or plaza anywhere on its footprint: affected RCI centres are outlined, with LV and C labels when zoomed in. A small nonmodal panel shows service status, affected-zone count, average total land value and additional land/commercial score due to this individual plot. Hover an outlined zone for unboosted base, total value, combined amenities and this plot. Counts of capped zones and missing transport explain lack of further benefit. Native single-tile Park works too. Details retains operation controls. Close, Escape or selecting another tool removes highlights. Selection also works with map overlays when inspect is already selected.

Uncapped source sums permit exact counterfactual contributions: current total minus what remains if only this plot is removed, respecting both amenity/global land caps. Multiple parks can jointly max a zone while each has zero additional marginal effect because neighbours still supply enough; the panel explicitly explains this. Growth-score benefits are not a claim of guaranteed population or stage growth; power/water/transport/demand/native scan checks still apply. Inspection caches across hover and recalculates on layout/field changes, with no simulation mutation.

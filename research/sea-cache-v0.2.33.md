# Sea pollution rendering cache

The running dense-city CPU capture in v0.2.32 identified `drawSeaPollution` as a hot path: it scans the whole map and issues a colored fill for every contaminated water tile on every rendered frame. The replacement retains a one-pixel-per-cell RGBA bitmap for each active city and composites it once. It scans current pollution bytes and water IDs to detect changes, uploads pixels only when they change, and stops drawing an entirely clean bitmap. It does not depend on city revision or array identity: monthly array replacement, in-place changes, water/shore edits and resized/new maps all invalidate correctly. The weak city cache does not serialize or change water/sewage accounting. A megacity bitmap is240x200, not1920x1600.

`tools/check-sea-cache.mjs` compares the original v0.2.32 function with the new one in the same Chromium context. Aligned0.5x/1x/2x images match every channel. In-place pollution, removed/added water, monthly byte-array replacement, changed shore, city resize and complete clearance also match. Repeated unchanged draws perform no pixel upload. The48,000-cell all-polluted isolated fixture measured median17.7ms before and0.1ms after (p9518.7ms/0.2ms). These are CPU/submission timings for this specific layer, not total frame time or device FPS.

At arbitrary fractional zoom the bitmap removes the former per-rectangle antialiasing grid. A uniform polluted sea remains uniform instead of showing seams between identical cells. A deliberately seam-heavy1.375x comparison differs in203382 of2304000 channels; the before/after image was inspected and shows the grid removed. Exact pixel parity is claimed only for the aligned cases above. Original color calculation, water-only mask and all simulation bytes are retained.

Full-game dense1833-zone night/rain captures, including baseline routing of only the original sea drawing function, are retained separately. Rendering/UI checks also cover realistic art with/without weather, contamination, night lights, pause and session reload. Full source gameplay/UI/audio and remaining custom systems are still in progress.

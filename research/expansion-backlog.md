This records requested scope, not implemented features. Public release notes identify what is actually playable. Preserve the original-ROM reconstruction objective alongside these explicit player extensions.

- Airports: municipal airport, smaller than the original 6×6 airport, light Cessna-style aircraft and private jets. International airport **24×18**, cargo aircraft and super-jumbo passenger aircraft. Military base adds fighters and supports industry. Aircraft need distinct routes/timing/capacity and operational gates, not only different sprites.
- Parks: pocket garden 1×1, neighbourhood park 2×2, city park 4×4, civic plaza 3×3; local land-value and citizen-happiness benefits, upkeep and readable inspection/coverage. In development after utilities v0.2.22.
- Regional economy: road, rail, sea and air freight depots; city imports/exports and inventories; prices driven by regional supply/demand. Requested goods: **oil, drugs, plastics, metals, fertilizers, components, gold, diamonds, uranium, contraband, weapons**. Treat all as fictional game commodities; no real-world production instructions.
- Finite, geographically distributed regional deposits, varied between regions/cities; mines/extraction facilities, factories and production chains. Persistent inventories, reserves, price history, storage, capacity, trade costs/income and connection requirements. Trade and production must conserve stocks and integrate with save/session/region data and budget inspection. No real-time web market data needed: this is a simulated economy.

Security/hazmat, bomb squad and 2×2 FBI/CIA/counterterrorism offices; fictional rare incidents and disaster-menu entry. Mega-tsunami/asteroid/nuclear/biological disasters. High-density roads and elevated monorail/stations over roads. See canonical private branch `feature/civic-expansion` and `research/civic-expansion-active.md`.
- Original gifts: restore remaining source-derived unlock conditions and names, preserve original inventory constraints but explain queue in tray. The earlier source trace is useful; current limited gift implementation is not full coverage.
- Full original game: remaining simulation/actor/UI/sequence/SPC/DSP parity and original-runtime comparisons. Do not equate these Turbo features or partial verification with a complete faithful port.
